Questions

Do I need my own WhatsApp number?

Yes, a WhatsApp Business number registered with Meta's Cloud API. It can't be in use on the WhatsApp app at the same time. We help you set it up.

Are WhatsApp's own charges included?

Meta charges separately for some WhatsApp conversations, especially ones your business starts. Replies to customers who message you first are usually free within 24 hours.

What counts as a conversation?

One chat with one customer. On WhatsApp, messages from the same customer within 24 hours count as one conversation.

Can the assistant make phone calls?

Phone calls are coming in the next phase. Today the assistant handles WhatsApp text and voice notes, and website chat and voice.
