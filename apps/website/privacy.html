Privacy notice

Draft for review. This notice must be reviewed by a lawyer against India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules before launch.

Who we are

vanibot provides AI chat and voice assistants to businesses ("our customers"). When you talk to an assistant on a business's website or WhatsApp number, that business decides why your data is used, and vanibot processes it on their behalf.

What we collect

Messages and voice notes you send to an assistant, the details you choose to share (such as your name and phone number), and basic technical data needed to deliver the service.

How it is used

To answer your questions, to pass your request to the business you contacted when you agree, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell personal data.

Service providers

Messages are processed by AI and speech providers that may be located outside India. WhatsApp messages pass through Meta. Data is stored in India.

How long we keep it

Conversations are deleted after the retention period set by the business (one year by default). Leads stay with the business until it deletes them.

Your choices

You can ask the business you contacted, or us, to access, correct or delete your data. Contact: privacy@vanibot.example (placeholder).
